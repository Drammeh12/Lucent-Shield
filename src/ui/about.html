Security beyond boundaries.

Most security tools hand you a wall of jargon and leave you to figure out what it means. Lucent Shield does the opposite. It watches your websites and your code, tells you in plain words what is exposed, and spells out exactly how to fix it.

What it does

White-box scanning

Point it at your own code and it looks for leaked secrets, risky patterns, and dependencies with known vulnerabilities.

Black-box scanning

Give it a website you own and it checks the outside view: headers, TLS, cookies, and files that should never be public.

Live protection

A padlock that stays green and calm when you are safe, and turns red the moment something changes, so you are never the last to know.

Forensics

Inspect a file or an artifact, capture its fingerprint, and surface anything that looks out of place.

How we are different

Plenty of scanners can list problems. Lucent Shield is built to keep watching after the first scan, to explain itself in language a founder can act on, and to show you the fix next to the finding. It is a defensive tool. It only ever looks at what you own or are allowed to test, and it never attacks anything.

Who builds it

Lucent Shield is a product of Bash Lucent, where we build, deploy, and secure real software for real people. It sits alongside our other work, from Bash Cloud to Naija Campus, under one simple idea: good technology should protect the people who use it.
