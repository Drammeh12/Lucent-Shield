Privacy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Lucent Shield is built to protect you, so it collects as little as it can. This page explains what it keeps and why.

What we store

For your account we store your name, your email address, when you created the account and when you last logged in. We also store the assets you choose to protect, their scan results, the alerts those scans produce, and a record of each scan you authorised (when, and from which internet address), so there is always proof that a test was permitted. Only you can see your assets and results. We do not sell any of it, and we do not share it with anyone.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. That permanently removes your account, your assets, their scan results, your alerts and your activity. We keep only the records that you authorised each scan (when, from which internet address, and for which target), because they are the legal proof that every test was permitted.

Your password

Your password is never stored. We keep only a one-way scrambled form of it (a salted scrypt hash), which is enough to check it when you log in but cannot be turned back into the password, not even by us.

Security records

To protect accounts, we record log-in and sign-up attempts with the email used, the internet address and the time. The person who runs this Lucent Shield service can see these records, the list of accounts, and the authorization records, so they can stop misuse.

Scanning other sites

When you scan a website, Lucent Shield makes ordinary, read-only requests to it, the same kind a normal visitor's browser makes. To show a site's icon in the dashboard, it fetches that icon through a public icon service. It never sends your scan results anywhere outside your instance.

Alerts to other tools

If you choose to connect an alerts webhook, such as Slack or Discord, Lucent Shield will send a short alert message there when a watched asset changes. That is the only time information leaves your instance, and only because you asked it to.

Cookies

Lucent Shield uses one essential cookie to keep you logged in for up to 24 hours after you log in. There is no tracking and no advertising.

Contact

For anything about your data, reach Bash Lucent through the contact details on our main site.
